01 · OUR STORY

Your Everyday Spot For Fresh Chai & Good Times

Jay Somnath Tea Store is a vibrant neighborhood destination in Nikol. Whether you are starting your morning with hot Rajwadi Chai, grabbing a chilled Pepsi or Red Bull, or catching up with friends, we have everything you need under one roof.

Our fresh Gujarati farsan — including Gathiya, Fafda, Bhajiya, Jalebi, and Dalvada — is prepared fresh daily in pure groundnut oil (Shuddh Singtel) to ensure authentic traditional taste.
